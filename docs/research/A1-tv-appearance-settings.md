# A1: TV appearance and accessibility display settings
 
Checked 2026-11-20. Tags: [C] I read the maker's page, [S] secondary source, [M] memory. Each tag links to its page. NOT FOUND = nothing on the maker's pages I could open. Menus vary by model and software version.
 
## Apple tvOS
| Setting | What it changes | Options | Menu path |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light/Dark/Automatic | Backgrounds, menus | Light, Dark, Automatic. Automatic: Light in daytime, Dark at night. Clock, sunset or sensor: NOT FOUND; a blog says "time of day" [[S]](https://9to5mac.com/?p=486186) | Settings > General > Appearance [[C]](https://support.apple.com/en-us/guide/tv/atvb46af4f11/tvos) |
| Item size | Home Screen icons | Default, Large (5 icons per row, not 6) | Same [[C]](https://support.apple.com/en-us/guide/tv/atvb46af4f11/tvos) |
| Text size, Bold Text | Text | Slider; on/off | Settings > Accessibility > Display [[C]](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/display-bold-text-or-change-the-text-size-atvb0222141c/tvos) |
| Increase Contrast, Reduce Transparency, Focus Style | Colour contrast; blur on backgrounds; highlight | On/off; on/off; High Contrast | Settings > Accessibility > Display [[C]](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/increase-screen-contrast-atvb81aeaf3f/tvos) |
| Color Filters | Whole screen colours | Grayscale, Red/Green, Green/Red, Blue/Yellow; intensity; Color Tint [[C]](https://support.apple.com/HT207032) | Settings > Accessibility > Display > Color Filters [[C]](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/atvbf413b243/tvos) |
| Reduce Motion | Navigation animation | Reduce Motion on/off; Auto-Play Video Previews on/off | Settings > Accessibility > Motion [[C]](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/reduce-screen-motion-atvb1f949820/tvos) |
| Accent colours | | NOT FOUND | |

## LG webOS
| Setting | What it changes | Options | Menu path |
|---|---|---|---|

| High Contrast | Menu backgrounds, font colours | On | General > Accessibility [[S]](https://manuals.plus/_lg/webos-g3-77-inch-4k-ultra-hd-oled-smart-tv-manual); function [[C]](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/navigating-on-screen-display-menu-television--20154712691472) |
| Grayscale | Menus in black and white | On/Off | webOS 6.0+: All Settings > General > Accessibility. webOS 5.0: All Settings > Accessibility [[C]](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-tv-how-to-adjust-the-grayscale-feature--20153258853854) |
