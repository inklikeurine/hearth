# A6 – OLED burn-in: what LG, Samsung and Sony say
 
**Answer:** All three warn that logos, menus and similar fixed elements can leave permanent marks on an OLED, and all three build in shifting, dimming or refresh functions. LG is the most reassuring. Sony lists no logo-specific dimming. None of the pages I read names clocks.
 
Quotes are short fragments, not full sentences. Only each maker's own pages used.
 
| Maker | What they say about static images | What the TV does to protect itself | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| LG | Burn-in may follow when "a static image, such as a channel logo, is displayed for an extended period." LG also calls retention rare in normal viewing. | **Screen Shift** moves pixels in the static area. **Logo Luminance Adjustment** dims detected logos. **TPC** dims stationary images judged high-risk. **Pixel Refresher** runs at power-off after 4 hours of total use; Pixel Cleaning can be run by hand (about an hour, TV plugged in). | [Support: Pixel Cleaning](https://www.lg.com/in/support/product-support/troubleshoot/help-library/cs-CT52006584-20154768398494/), [OLED reliability](https://www.lg.com/hk_en/tv/oled-tv/oled-reliability/) |
| Samsung | OLEDs risk "potential screen burn-in or image retention when displaying static images" for long periods. | **Pixel Shift** moves pixels at intervals (screen edges may slip off-screen). **Auto Logo Brightness** lowers brightness on fixed logos or captions (Low/High). **Screen Saver** starts after 2 minutes of the same still image. **Pixel Refresh** runs with the TV off for over an hour. | [Support](https://www.samsung.com/hk_en/support/tv-audio-video/how-to-troubleshoot-burn-in-or-image-retention-on-your-samsung-oled-tv/) |
| Sony | OLEDs "may experience permanent image retention if an image is continuously displayed." The page names on-screen menus, program guides, channel logos and tickers. | **Pixel shift** (must be switched on). **Screen saver** after idle time. Brightness gradually lowered for risky content. **Panel refresh** and **panel calibration** run automatically. | [Support](https://www.sony.co.in/electronics/support/articles/00173479) |
 
## Not confirmed
- LG's US help pages would not load; the support link is India's. Screen Shift and logo dimming come from LG's reliability page, not a help-library page.
- Samsung is the Hong Kong page; Sony is the India page. Features vary by model and region (Samsung says so).
- Nothing tested on real panels.
